The narrow candidate worth testing is a **folded-query high-degree typed OLE**
(`FQ-HD-tOLE`) specialized to the way C4 consumes boundary authentication.
It does not attempt to manufacture an ordinary degree-1 `Fp2` MAC for every
`u16` and carry bit. Instead, setup fixes and commits the Packed16 masks, and
the response authenticates only the Fiat--Shamir linear functionals that C4
actually opens.

This is the useful seam because the eligible C4 boundaries first enter the
proof through `open_matrix_p`, `cache_fold_rows_p`, `cache_fold_cols_p`, or
their weighted linear variants. K is then paired with an ordinary Q claim, V
with an ordinary softmax claim, and a chain-exit value with an ordinary
weight/residual claim. The current C4 path has no typed-by-typed product. Thus
degree grows only after the large vector has been folded, and by at most one.
This call-graph condition is part of the candidate and must remain true.

## Folded Packed16 identity

Let `B = 2^16`, `H = 2^15`, and use the existing Packed16 correction

```text
c_i = e_i + (1 - 2 e_i) b_i
x_i = a_i + d_i - H - B c_i.
```

For every public coefficient vector `r`, including every C4 MLE equality
vector,

```text
<r,x>
  = <r,a>
  + <r,d - H - B e>
  - B <r * (1 - 2 e), b>.
```

Thus one corrected boundary opening needs exactly two private typed queries:
`<r,a>` and `<r*(1-2e),b>`. Everything else is public and transcript-bound.
No element-wise arithmetic lift is algebraically necessary.

## Candidate construction

1. Setup authenticates only short random bit seeds under the connection's
   existing verifier-only `Delta in Fp2`.
2. A domain-separated public XOF derives the local-PRG hypergraphs. No
   hypergraph is serialized per output.
3. The seed is expanded with the `XOR4-MAJ7` candidate used by Agarwal--Baum--
   Braun--Scholl: seed length `1024`, output length `2^20`, and arithmetic
   degree at most `11`. Setup precomputes the prover's high-degree polynomial
   shares for packed `a` and `b`; it may store them response by response.
4. The response sends the unchanged Packed16 `d` words and `e` bitmap.
5. At each existing C4 boundary opening, a resident fused fold computes the
   two functionals above. Linear equalities remain degree 11. Where an
   existing GEMM closure multiplies a folded typed claim by an ordinary
   degree-1 claim, the corresponding QuickSilver term has degree at most 12.
6. Reuse the existing closure structure, but give its final zero/product
   accumulator the degree-12 representation. High-degree tags never enter a
   typed-by-typed product or a per-coordinate nonlinear closure.

### Executable live bridge

The existing C4 consumers require ordinary degree-1 MAC values. The minimal
integrated seam therefore sends one masked `Fp2` correction per live folded
opening and records its compact query description. All such corrections are
transcript-fixed before a single scalar challenge `beta` is sampled. The
response then forms

```text
sum_j beta^(j+1) * (typed_open_j - ordinary_open_j)
```

as one global query, performs one fused 12-lane CUDA fold, and closes the
aggregate at degree 12. Rayon reduces the public query descriptions in
parallel; lot expansion and the final fold are CUDA SIMT. The strict
implementation and codec reject zero bridges or more than `1,000,000`
bridges. This bridge traffic and the envelope framing are measured in the
actual proof; they are not hidden inside the `66,270,953-B` projection.
Typed K/V segments carry their public row count independently of the omitted
legacy per-cell keys, so stacked decode prefixes retain canonical geometry
without recreating C4 authentication material.

The specialization relative to general mixed arithmetic is load-bearing.
C4 needs no Boolean-field witness after the correction and no general
`F2 <-> Fp2` conversion. The second field, per-output degree reduction, and
per-output arithmetic lifts from a general daBit construction are omitted.

### ABI46 typed setup and lot producer

The next checkpoint implements the actual high-degree MAC convention rather
than zero timing buffers. A prover seed MAC is the degree-1 polynomial
`m + x X`; its evaluation at verifier-only `Delta` equals the verifier key.
The exact XOR4-MAJ7 predicate has degree 12, with the semantic output bit as
the top coefficient and the lower 12 coefficients stored in the prover slab.
The verifier expands only seed keys and never exposes `Delta` to the prover.

Public hypergraph rows use a statement-bound ChaCha8 XOF stream per
`(expansion, output)` and reject duplicate indices until 11 distinct positions
exist. This makes incidence generation independently SIMT while remaining
bit-exact against the CPU oracle. ABI46 supplies separate prover and verifier
CUDA lot kernels and retains the ABI45 resident fused response fold. One byte
per generated `b` bit avoids write races; the online Packed16 kernel will pack
the response bitmap.

## Security ledger

The current C4 anchor has `78.80929487391641` soundness bits. To keep the
union bound at or above 78 bits, all newly introduced failure terms together
must be at most

```text
2^-78 - 2^-78.80929487391641 = 2^-79.21980987002212.
```

For the Goldilocks quadratic extension, a degree-12 root bound is about
`12 / |Fp2| = 2^-124.41503749860703` per independent batch. This leaves ample
statistical margin. The executable accounting combines five response closes
and the degree-2 setup close as `62 / |Fp2| = 2^-122.04580368894132`.

Every response also uses one post-correction scalar-power bridge batch. Lean
proves that a fixed list with a nonzero error has at most `K` bad `beta`
values. The codec and both protocol roles enforce `K <= 1,000,000`; a union
over five prepared responses is therefore at most
`5,000,000 / |Fp2| = 2^-105.74650333511666`.

The computational term is owner-admitted, not proved. The 2025 SoK lists
`XOR4-MAJ7(1024, 2^20)` as a 128-bit cryptanalysis challenge rather than a
proved primitive. Under the named `XOR4-MAJ7-128` assumption, a conservative union
over all 253 expansions would still leave about `120.017 bits`, above the
96-bit component target. Combining that term, the bridge batch, all close
terms and the C4 anchor gives `78.80929486268863` bits, above the 78-bit floor.
This is a conditional security claim, not an unconditional reduction. Earlier Goldreich parameters were broken far below their
claims, and the 2024 bit-fixing attack specifically warns that resiliency and
algebraic immunity are insufficient for XOR-MAJ predicates. Before credit:

- run or conservatively reproduce the current predicate-specific attack
  estimates for the exact public-incidence generation and 253-instance use;
- account for multi-instance/data loss across the complete five-response
  vector;
- show both total distinguishing advantage (weight hiding) and all added
  soundness terms are at most `2^-79.21980987002212`; target at least 96 bits
  for the new computational component rather than sitting on the floor;
- specify both corrupt-party cases, abort leakage, setup commitment binding,
  one-time allocation/burn, and external-`Delta` composition.

No 78-bit claim may be based only on the paper's intended 128-bit label.

### Last cryptanalytic estimates — 2026-08-29

The May 2026 D'Antona--Meaux--Unal common-bias paper is newer than the SoK and
directly attacks `THR XOR` Goldreich PRGs. Its exact finite hypergeometric
formula and selection algorithm were implemented in the executable checker.
For our notation its threshold side is `MAJ7` (`a=7,t=4`) and its linear side
is `XOR4`.

| Exact screen | Best result |
| --- | ---: |
| Common-bias, no selection | `364.889897276 bits`, `lambda=338` |
| Common-bias, one selection | `284.895686053 bits`, `lambda=258`, `m'=187,539` |
| Same attack after union over 253 seeds | `276.912692478 bits` |
| 2024 Group-and-Solve small-majority condition | `1024 > (7-2+1)^2+2 = 38`; only constant, not exponential, speedup |

The selection depth is exactly one: from all `2^20` outputs the greedy bound
gives `ceil(2^20*7/1024)=7,168` rows sharing one majority input, then only
`ceil(7,168*6/1,023)=43`, below the minimum `1,025` equations needed for a
second selected input. Thus the 2026 attack that breaks the large-majority SoK
parameters does **not** come close to the C4.1 component target.

This remains a negative test, not certification. The same 2026 paper selects
new 128-bit challenge predicates by requiring algebraic immunity at least
`ceil(8s+1)=17`, resilience at least `ceil(5s)=10`, and common bias below
`2^-128` for `s=2`. Exhaustive truth-table calculation gives XOR4-MAJ7
algebraic immunity `4` and resilience `4`, so it does not meet that newer
conservative selection rule even though the paper's new attack does not break
it. No published reduction found in this review supplies the missing lower
bound.

For the absolute 78-bit composition floor, a single-instance PRG claim would
need at least

```text
79.21980987002212 + log2(253) = 87.20280344471643 bits.
```

To retain the internal 96-bit component reserve it needs `103.9829935746943`
single-instance bits. The owner-admitted `XOR4-MAJ7-128` assumption clears
both. The security gate is therefore **CONDITIONALLY PASS**; revoking or
invalidating the assumption reopens it immediately.

## Distinction from rejected nearby ideas

- Scholl's low-communication random `1-out-of-N` OT does not give a free
  affine programmed table; chosen-message conversion reintroduces output
  corrections.
- Packing several witnesses into one field correction loses arbitrary C4
  linear openings unless an additional binding proof reconstructs them.
- C6's grand residual projects witness-dependent correction relations after
  the fact. Here `a,b` are setup-fixed and committed before Fiat--Shamir, and
  `d,e` are public before the query; arbitrary query coefficients are handled
  by the committed typed-query interface.
- General ABBS mixed conversion authenticates the same seed in two fields.
  This candidate deliberately keeps only the `Fp2` side because C4 consumes
  no Boolean-field result.
